## 🔬 Part 2: Technical Abstract

Traditional behavioral predictive models and large language models (LLMs) frequently experience context drift and misalignment when decoding human communications characterized by non-linear, sub-textual, or defensive compliance protocols ('masking'). This paper introduces the **Two-Processor Human Emulation (TPHE)** framework, a novel prompt-layer architecture that bifurcates human behavioral analysis into a dual-engine processing matrix.

* **Processor 1 (The Somato-Biochemical Engine):** Extracts latent environmental and physiological stressors from the input text, transforming these non-linear vectors into dynamic probability weights.
* **Processor 2 (The Central Executive Core):** Subsequently executes traditional binary and symbolic logic calculations through the weighted filters generated by Processor 1. 

By calculating the exact chemical coefficient weighting an seemingly irrational human input prior to execution, TPHE eliminates standard analytical ambiguity.
